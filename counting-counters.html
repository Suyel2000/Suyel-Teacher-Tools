How to Use Counting Counters

🔢 Default Array of 10s:

  • Counters automatically order in clean arrays of 10 counters per row with a subitizing gap between the 5th and 6th counters.
  • Click Arrange (Array of 10s) at any time to instantly organize scattered counters into groups of tens.

🎯 General Controls:

  • Click ➕ Add Counter or ➕ Add 10 to quickly build large sets.
  • Click & Drag single counters or draw a marquee box to multi-select.
  • Double-click a counter to flip it over (Two-Color counters).
