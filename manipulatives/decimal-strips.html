How to Use Decimal Strips

🎯 Main Objective: Explore decimal values, fractions, percentages, and place value through interactive visual strips.

1. Drag & Drop Tiles: Drag colorful decimal tiles from the bottom drawer onto any decimal strip.

2. Change Divisions: Toggle between Whole, Tenths (0.1), Hundredths (0.01), and Thousandths (0.001) grid overlays to visualize precision.

3. Observe Overflow: If the total sum on a strip exceeds 1.0, the total badge turns red with an overflow warning!

4. Side Inspector: Select any strip to see its equivalent Fraction, Percentage, Expanded Place Value, and 10x10 Visual Grid representation.

5. Interactive Modes: Switch to Practice or Quiz tabs on the right side to test your decimal math skills with interactive scoring!
