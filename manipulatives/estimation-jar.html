❓ How to Use Estimation Jar

Overview: Estimation Jar helps students in Classes 1–5 build spatial visual skills, number sense, and estimation strategies.

Key Strategies:

  • Grouping / Benchmark: Look at a small cluster of 5 or 10 objects, then see how many similar clusters fit inside the jar.
  • Grid Partitioning: Click the 💡 Hint button to overlay grid lines. Estimate one section and multiply!
  • Layer Stacking: Count the bottom layer and multiply by the estimated height in layers.
