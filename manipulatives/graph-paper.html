📐 How to Use Graph Paper

📍 Plotting Points: Select the Point tool and click on the grid. Points automatically auto-label (A, B, C...) and snap to coordinates.


📏 Drawing Lines & Shapes: Choose Line, Segment, Ray, Polygon, or Shape tool. Click on the canvas to place vertices. Click initial point to close polygon.


🖐️ Pan & Zoom: Use mouse wheel or pinch gesture to zoom in/out. Drag canvas with Select tool or middle mouse button to pan infinitely.


🔄 Transformations: Select an object and use sliders in the left sidebar to Translate, Rotate, or Reflect dynamically!


🏆 Challenges: Switch on Challenge Mode to test coordinate skills and earn scores!
