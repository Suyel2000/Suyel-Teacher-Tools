How to Use Tally Master

• Add/Remove: Tap ➕/➖ buttons to add individual or groups of tallies.

• Automatic Grouping: Every 5th tally automatically converts into a diagonal slash crossing 4 vertical lines.

• Practice Mode: Generate a target number and build the matching tallies!

• Challenge Mode: Look at the rendered tallies and test your fast-counting skills under a timer.

• Draw Mode: Tap or drag your finger across the tally area to naturally draw marks!
