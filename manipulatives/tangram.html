🧩 Tangram Studio Guide


Controls:

  • Drag: Click and drag pieces to position them.
  • Rotate: Select a piece and use bottom toolbar buttons or press 'R' key.
  • Flip: Select the Parallelogram piece and press 'F' or click Flip button.
  • Magnetic Snap: Pieces automatically snap when close to target outline or other aligned pieces.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Arrow keys (Move), R (Rotate +45°), F (Flip), Tab (Cycle pieces), Esc (Deselect).
